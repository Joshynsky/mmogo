Private by design

Your money stays yours.

  • No account, no serverEntries live in a database on your phone. mmogo sends none of them anywhere.
  • Internet only to check for updatesThe only permission mmogo asks Android for is internet, used only to check for updates, when you open the app and at most once a week after a successful check. GitHub sees your IP address and that the request comes from the mmogo app. No entries, names or messages are sent. You can switch it off on the Updates page (Profile, Settings, Updates, or the bell); when it is off, mmogo makes no request.
  • Reads only the message you pastemmogo reads the SMS you paste and picks out the amount, receiver, code, date and cost. It has no access to your inbox or any other message.
  • Never saves your M-Pesa balanceThe balance in the message is ignored. It isn't picked out, saved or shown anywhere, and the message text itself isn't kept either.

Questions

Before you install.

Is it safe to install?

The app asks Android for one permission, internet, used only to check for updates (you can switch that off in the app), and the source code is public on GitHub. Because it's not on the Play Store, check that your download matches the SHA-256 above.

What if I lose my phone or uninstall the app?

Your data is only on your phone, and uninstalling the app erases it. Android's own backup is off, and Android's phone-to-phone transfer will not carry mmogo's data. In version 0.1.1 you can make a backup file yourself (Profile, Settings, Backup and restore) and restore it later, merging it in or replacing what is on the phone. The file is not encrypted and holds names and phone numbers, so keep it somewhere private. mmogo can also save a backup to a folder you pick after every few entries.

Is there an iPhone version?

Not yet. mmogo is Android only for now.

Does it connect to my M-Pesa account?

No. It never touches your M-Pesa account or PIN. It only reads the M-Pesa messages you paste in, and uses the parts it needs.
