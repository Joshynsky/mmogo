How it works

From a text message to a clear picture.

Swipe through, or use the arrows. Slides 1 to 5 are the standard version; the auto-read version starts with a notification instead.

1 of 8

Copy the M-Pesa SMS

Long-press the confirmation message in your messages app and copy it. The standard version can't see your inbox, so it only ever works with the message you paste.

2 of 8

Paste it in

Tap Parse M-Pesa message on the Add screen and paste. Send Money, Paybill and Buy Goods messages all work.

3 of 8

Check what it filled in

The amount, code, date, receiver and transaction cost are filled in for you. Look it over, then Review & save.

4 of 8

Sort it, or log cash

Pick a category such as Groceries or Rent, or make your own. Paid cash? Add that too, so your total is complete.

5 of 8

See where it went

Totals for the month, a bar for each type of payment, the cost of the transactions, and your latest entries.

6 of 8

See it by day, week, month or year

Pick a length and a period, tap a bar for its total and daily average, and see which types of payment took the most.

7 of 8

See who you pay most

Paid to ranks the people, paybills and shops you pay, with search, filter and sort. The same phone number written two ways counts as one person.

8 of 8

Auto-read asks first

In the auto-read version mmogo asks before it reads anything. It keeps only messages from M-Pesa and does not send any of it off your phone. Reading is off until you switch it on, and you can turn it off again in Settings.

What's new

What's new in 0.1.2

Six things changed. Swipe through, or read them all on the News page.

New

A second version: auto-read

mmogo auto-read reads your M-Pesa messages as they arrive and puts them on a waiting list. A notification tells you one is ready; it shows no amount and no name. Tap it to parse, then categorize. It asks for SMS permission and your consent, and it is off until you switch it on. The standard version works without any of it.

Messages

Withdrawals, airtime and Pochi

mmogo now understands these M-Pesa messages. A withdrawal is money out, but not money you spent, so it adds nothing to what you spent. Its transaction cost is still tracked.

Calendar

Weeks

A week now runs from the day you choose in Settings, Monday unless you change it. Past weekly figures are regrouped to match; nothing saved changes.

Paid to

Same phone number = one person

0712 345 678 and +254 712 345 678 now count as one person on Paid to, and their totals are added together. A 07 number and a 01 number are different numbers and stay separate.

Bell

Notifications

The bell now opens Notifications, where updates and notices appear and anything new shows on the bell. In the auto-read version your M-Pesa messages wait there too. The Updates page is now in Settings, under About.

Your data

Backups and privacy

Backups from 0.1.1 still restore; a backup made with 0.1.2 cannot be restored by 0.1.1 or older. Uninstalling mmogo erases your data from this phone. The standard version asks only for internet, used only to check for updates (switchable off). The auto-read version also asks for SMS and notification permission, only if you switch reading on.

Private by design

Your money stays yours.

  • No account, no serverEntries live in a database on your phone. mmogo sends none of them anywhere.
  • Internet only to check for updatesThe standard version asks Android for one permission, internet, used only to check for updates. The auto-read version also asks for SMS and notification permissions, but only if you switch reading on. The update check runs when you open the app and at most once a week after a successful check. GitHub sees your IP address and that the request comes from the mmogo app. No entries, names or messages are sent. You can switch it off on the Updates page (Profile, Settings, About, Updates); when it is off, mmogo makes no request.
  • Standard version: reads only the message you pastemmogo reads the SMS you paste and picks out the amount, receiver, code, date and cost. It has no access to your inbox or any other message.
  • Auto-read version: only M-Pesa messages, only on this phoneIf you choose the auto-read version and switch reading on, it asks for SMS and notification permission and for your consent. It keeps only messages from M-Pesa, holds them on your phone until you parse them or they expire (up to 24 hours), and its notification shows no amount and no name. Your messages are not sent anywhere.
  • Other people's names and numbersM-Pesa messages contain the names and phone numbers of people you send money to or receive it from. mmogo handles them only on your phone, and nothing leaves it.
  • Never saves your M-Pesa balanceThe balance in the message is ignored. It isn't picked out, saved or shown anywhere, and the message text itself isn't kept either.

Questions

Before you install.

Which version should I pick?

Pick the standard version if you are happy to paste each M-Pesa message yourself. Pick the auto-read version if you want mmogo to notice new M-Pesa messages as they arrive and let you parse them with one tap on a notification. The auto-read version asks for SMS and notification permission and your consent, and it is off until you switch it on in Settings. If you do not want to give SMS permission, use the standard version.

Is it safe to install?

The standard version asks Android for one permission, internet, used only to check for updates (you can switch that off in the app). The auto-read version also asks for SMS and notification permissions, only if you switch reading on, and keeps only messages from M-Pesa on your phone. The source code is public on GitHub. Because it's not on the Play Store, check that your download matches the SHA-256 above.

The download will not start from WhatsApp, Facebook or another app

The built-in browser inside other apps usually cannot download an app file. Open this page in Chrome instead: tap the menu (the three dots or the share icon) and choose "Open in Chrome" or "Open in browser", or copy the page link and paste it into Chrome.

Can I switch between the two versions?

Yes. Both versions are signed the same way, so you can install either one over the other and your entries stay on the phone. Making a backup first (Profile, Settings, Backup and restore) is a good habit before any install.

What if I lose my phone or uninstall the app?

Your data is only on your phone, and uninstalling the app erases it. Android's own backup is off, and Android's phone-to-phone transfer will not carry mmogo's data. Since version 0.1.1 you can make a backup file yourself (Profile, Settings, Backup and restore) and restore it later, merging it in or replacing what is on the phone. The file is not encrypted and holds names and phone numbers, so keep it somewhere private. mmogo can also save a backup to a folder you pick after every few entries.

Is there an iPhone version?

Not yet. mmogo is Android only for now.

Does it connect to my M-Pesa account?

No. It never touches your M-Pesa account or PIN. The standard version only reads the M-Pesa messages you paste in. The auto-read version, once you switch it on, reads M-Pesa messages as they arrive, on your phone. Both use only the parts they need.

Who makes it?

mmogo is an independent project by Joshua Waga. It isn't made by, or connected to, Safaricom or M-Pesa.
